Privacy Policy

Meta application “Stelele canta” · last updated 28 August 2026

This policy covers the Meta application “Stelele canta” (App ID 1364407472553557), a private, single-operator tool used to publish and measure content for the Facebook Page Stelele Cântă and the Instagram account @stelelecanta. It is not offered to the public, and it has no users other than its operator.

What the application accesses

Only content and statistics belonging to the operator’s own Facebook Page and Instagram account:

  • publishing videos (Reels) to that Page and that Instagram account;
  • reading view, reach and retention statistics for those same posts.

Meta provides these statistics in aggregate form only. The application does not receive, request or store the identity of any viewer.

What it does not do

  • It offers no login to any third party; no other person can connect an account to it.
  • It does not read private messages, comments, or friend/follower lists.
  • It does not access any Page, account or profile other than the operator’s own.
  • It uses no advertising features, builds no audiences, and performs no profiling.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone.

Where data is stored

The application runs entirely on the operator’s own computer in Germany. Published videos and the aggregate statistics described above are stored locally on that machine. There is no third-party server, no cloud database, and no analytics or tracking service. This webpage itself sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored statistics are kept solely for the operator’s own review and may be deleted at any time. Because the application holds no personal data belonging to any other person, there is nothing for a third party to request access to or deletion of. Any such request may nevertheless be sent to the address above and will be answered.

Your rights under the GDPR (DSGVO)

Where personal data concerning you were processed, you would have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As set out above, this application processes no personal data relating to visitors or viewers.
